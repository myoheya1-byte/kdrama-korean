The First Snow

A bus stop on a cold evening. Two friends who secretly like each other.
A어? 눈 온다!
Eo? Nun onda!
Oh? It's snowing!
B진짜네. 첫눈이다.
Jinjjane. Cheonnunida.
You're right. It's the first snow.
A우와, 너무 예쁘다. 근데 너무 추워…
Uwa, neomu yeppeuda. Geunde neomu chuwo…
Wow, it's so pretty. But it's so cold…
B자, 내 목도리 해. 따뜻하게 입으라니까.
Ja, nae mokdori hae. Ttatteutage ibeuranikka.
Here, wear my scarf. I told you to dress warmly.
A고마워. 우리… 붕어빵 먹으러 갈래?
Gomawo. Uri… bungeoppang meogeureo gallae?
Thanks. Do you… want to go get some fish bread?
B좋아. 첫눈 오는 날엔 같이 있어야지.
Joa. Cheonnun oneun naren gachi isseoyaji.
Sure. We should be together on the day of the first snow.
